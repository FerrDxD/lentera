# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **LENTERA Score** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Use inclusive language.
* Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
* Critique ideas, not individuals.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting **security@lentera.test**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers retain the right to remove, edit, or ban offending content or participants from the project.
